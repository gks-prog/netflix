Meri Diya,

Pata nahi ye letter padhte waqt tum kya feel karogi… shayad muskuraogi, shayad chup ho jaogi, ya shayad meri tarah kuch der ke liye bas in lafzon ko dekhti rahogi.

Lekin aaj mujhe tumse kuch kehna hai....

Main tumse sirf isliye mohabbat nahi karta kyunki tum mere saath achha feel karti ho ya mujhe accha feel krwaati ho. ya uss raat ki wjh se jo kuch bhi hamare beech hua kyuki agr tum mera sbse pehle letter bhi pdhoge jo maine aapke birthday pe diya tha mtlb usse pehle 7 jun 2026. toh you will get the context aache se i guess.

Main tumse isliye mohabbat karta hoon kyunki tumhari rooh mein ek ajeeb si masoomiyat hai… ek softness hai, jo shayad tum khud bhi har waqt nahi dekh paati.

Mujhe tumhari woh hasi pasand hai jo bina kisi wajah ke aa jaati hai. Tumhara bachpana pasand hai. Tumhara overthink karna bhi… haan, kabhi-kabhi bahut pareshan karta hai mujhe, lekin usmein bhi mujhe tumhari parwah dikhai deti hai.

Aur mujhe tumhari woh side sabse zyada pyaari hai jo duniya se chhup jaati hai.
Woh Diya jo thak jaati hai.
Woh Diya jo sabko samajhne ki koshish karti hai, chahe khud andar se kitni bhi pareshaan ho.
Woh Diya jo kabhi-kabhi apne hi dil ki baat samajh nahi paati.
Woh Diya jo future ko lekar darr jaati hai.
Woh Diya jo kabhi-kabhi sochti hai ki shayad woh kisi ko utna pyaar nahi de paayegi jitna saamne wala usse deta hai.
Uss Diya se bhi mujhe utni hi mohabbat hai.

Tum jo pyaar mere liye feel krte ho use har roz prove karne ki zarurat nahi hai.
Tumhe mujhe utna hi pyaar karna zaroori nahi jitna main tumhen karta hoon.
Tumhe mere saamne hamesha strong banne ki zarurat nahi hai.
Aur sabse zaroori… tumhe mere saath rehne ke liye perfect hone ki zarurat nahi hai.

Main tumse koi perfect version nahi maangta. Mujhe tum chahiye. Apni saari uljhanon ke saath. Apni khamoshiyon ke saath. Apne sabhi darr ke saath. Apni hasi ke saath. Apne gusse ke saath. Apne bachpane ke saath. Apni muskurahat ke saath...

Kabhi-kabhi mujhe lagta hai tum khud ko bahut hi Jayda judge karti ho. Tum apne dil se poochti rehti ho,
"Kya main ise utna pyaar karti hoon?"
"Kya main mai yeh sb thik toh kr rhi hu na?"
"Kya hum sach mein hamesha saath rahenge?"

Sach kahun? Mere paas in sab sawaalon ka future se likha hua jawab nahi hai. Main ye promise nahi karunga ki zindagi mein kabhi kuch mushkil nahi hoga.. Lekin main itna zaroor keh sakta hoon... jab tak hum dono ek doosre ko Sacche dil se choose karte rahenge, Tb tak hum saath h.. main tumhara haath chhodne ki jaldi mein nahi hoon.

Mujhe tumhare saath sirf khoobsurat din nahi chahiye. Mujhe woh ordinary din bhi chahiye. Jahan hum bina kisi special reason ke saath baithe hon. Jahan tum mujhe kisi chhoti si baat par daant rahi ho. Jahan main tumhe dekh kar bina bataye muskura raha houn. Jahan main tumhe dekh kar bina reason ke aise hi ro rha hu kyuki tum khoobsurat ho...... Jahan hum dono apne apne kaam mein busy hain, phir bhi ek doosre ki presence se sukoon mil raha hai...

Mujhe woh zindagi chahiye jo Instagram par perfect nahi dikhti… par andar se ghar jaisi lagti hai.

Aur Diya… agar kabhi tumhe lage ki tum mere pyaar ka jawab nahi de paa rahi ho, toh please khud ko dosh mat dena. Pyaar koi hisaab nahi hai. Ye nahi hota ki maine tumhe 100 diya toh tumhe bhi 100 hi dena padega.

Kabhi main tumhe sambhalunga.
Kabhi tum mujhe sambhal lena.
Kabhi main zyada bolunga.
Kabhi tum bas mera haath pakad lena.
Kabhi main tumhe yakeen dilaunga.
Kabhi tum bas mere paas rehna.
Bas itna hi kaafi hai.

Aur ek baat jo shayad main tumse saamne kabhi itni achhe se nahi keh pata— Thank you. Meri zindagi mein aane ke liye. Mujhe apna dil dikhane ke liye, even when it scared you. Mujh par bharosa karne ke liye. Aur un chhoti-chhoti yaadon ke liye jo shayad tumhare liye normal moments honge, lekin mere liye woh meri zindagi ke woh tukde hain jinhe main baar-baar yaad karna chahta hoon.

Tum meri life mein sirf ek relationship banke nahi aayi. Tum meri kahaani ka woh hissa ban gayi ho jise main jaldi khatam nahi karna chahta. Aur agar kabhi tum apne aap ko meri nazron se dekh pao na… shayad tumhe samajh aaye ki tum kitni khoobsurat ho. Sirf chehre se nahi. Dil se.

Isliye nahi ki tum hamesha achha karti ho. Balki isliye ki tum galtiyan karke bhi achha rehne ki koshish karti ho. Aur mere liye, wahi tumhari sabse khoobsurat baat hai.

So, meri jaan… Aaj tumhe mujhse koi jawab dene ki zarurat nahi. Koi promise nahi. Koi assurance nahi. Bas ek cheez yaad rakhna— tumhe mujhse mohabbat karne ke liye apne aap ko badalne ki zarurat nahi hai.

Main tumhe samajhne ki koshish karta rahunga.
Tum bhi mujhe samajhne ki koshish karti rehna.
Aur baaki jo hum dono ke beech hai… use hum dono milkar jeeyenge. Aaj bhi. Kal bhi. Aur jitna kal humare naseeb mein likha hai, utna poore dil se.

I love you, Diya.
Tumhari khushi ke liye nahi, tumhari khoobsurti ke liye nahi, sirf un achhe moments ke liye nahi— main tumse tum hone ke liye mohabbat karta hoon.

Aur shayad meri mohabbat ka sabse sachcha matlab yahi hai—
“Tum jaisi ho, waise hi mere liye bahut ho.”

Always yours.
